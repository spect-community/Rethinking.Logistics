Strategy note structure (English, ~600–900 words of body text each):

1. **Principle** — the approach in two sentences.
2. **Why it matters** — the problem it answers (link challenges).
3. **Projects** — linked case studies with poster images.
4. **Key metrics** — table of metrics with values *as reported by students*, link to metric notes.
5. **Trade-offs** — from case-study trade-off sections.
6. **Evidence gaps** — metrics without tools, missing data (from the generated Evidence gaps page).
7. **Expert insights** — relevance-3 insights with source and evidence-status badges.
8. **Regulatory observations** — where current standards limit the approach (diplomatic framing).
9. **Questions for practice** — 3–5 open questions for developers, designers and municipalities.

**Highlights are not fixed in advance.** Which project, metric or insight leads each strategy is decided during writing from the curated base (relevance scores, data quality) and refined in later iterations. Claude proposes up to 3 highlight candidates per strategy with a one-line reason; Lukáš picks.

Strategies are written by Claude from the linked notes only; every number must trace to a case study, metric or insight note.
