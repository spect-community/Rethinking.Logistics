## The problem

Industry frameworks treat the warehouse as an asset. Spatial and urban quality has no metric on the investor side ([[Sustainability Unboxed – Delivering on Logistics and Distribution Warehouses]]). Industry partners see it differently on the ground: better logistics design can make negotiations with the city easier, and logistics is a lens on the city as a whole ([[Partner feedback – final review (2026)]]).

## What the evidence says

- [[Architects design assets; money lives at portfolio level]] — The stated point of a Big4 team addressing architecture students is to force a zoom-out: connect the asset the designer is drawing to the corporate level, and connect both to risk management.
- [[Investors chase tenant credit and forget the location]] — Assets bought for long e-commerce leases are now being re-examined: buyers over-weighted tenant credit and under-weighted where the building stood.
- [[We can price carbon, not nature]] — The profession has become good at calculating carbon and can now calculate biodiversity net gain, but has no way to value air cleaning, stormwater handling or community wellbeing — so nature has no monetary standing in the argument.
- [[Honest gap – valuing nature awaits better tools]] — Asked how to calculate carbon and runoff performance of a designed ecosystem, she declines to claim expertise and says she hopes emerging AI-based programs will help.
- [[Community engagement shortens permitting and leasing]] — Engaging communities early and mitigating traffic, pollution and green-space loss builds buy-in and can speed up development and leasing.
- [[A logistics-specific method would start from territory]] — An ideal methodology for logistics would reflect the scale, focus on territorial impact, evaluate impacts across categories, and centre transport — named as the biggest problem of this building type — rather than reusing a generic building framework.
- [[Speak their language or be invited too late]] — if you don't start speaking their language, you will not be invited to the conversation. You will be invited too late.
- [[KPIs as client-facing argument, not paperwork]] — Data and KPI analysis are described as a way to enforce design excellence internally and open dialogue with clients.
- [[Logistics is a lens on the whole city]] — Logistics is a good lens for understanding the city as a whole, not only one building type.
- [[Better logistics design eases negotiations with the city]] — Better logistics design can make negotiations with the city and authorities easier.
