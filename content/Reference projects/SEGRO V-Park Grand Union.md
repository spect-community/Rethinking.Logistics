## Project

SEGRO V-Park Grand Union is a multi-storey industrial building in Alperton, North West London, developed by SEGRO and St George (Berkeley Group) and completed in April 2024. It provides 134,500 sq ft over six storeys on a 1.7-acre site: two lower levels hold loading bays and parking, and four upper levels hold self-contained industrial units of various sizes, served by cargo lifts. Shared amenities include break-out and meeting rooms, a roof terrace and a reception service.

The building sits within the 22-acre Grand Union regeneration, where St George is building a mixed-use neighbourhood of 3,350 homes, 35% of them affordable. It targets BREEAM Excellent and has photovoltaic cells, an internal green wall and 20 charging points for electric cars and vans.
