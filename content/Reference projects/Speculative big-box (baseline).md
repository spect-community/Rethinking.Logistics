## Project

A generic reference for the standard speculative warehouse that most European logistics parks are built from. It is not a specific building. It exists so that the other reference projects and the student case studies can be compared with what is normally built.

## Typical characteristics

- Built before a tenant is known, to a standard specification that suits many possible users
- Single storey, large clear span, high clear height
- Docks on one or two sides, large paved yard and parking
- Sustainability certificate (often BREEAM or LEED) as a market requirement
- Sold by the developer at or before completion to a long-term investor
