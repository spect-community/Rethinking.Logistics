## Project

BESTSELLER Logistics Center West (LCW) is a 155,000 m² logistics centre for the fashion company BESTSELLER in Lelystad, on the Dutch Flevopolder, designed by Henning Larsen. The architect describes it as Europe's largest timber logistics centre. It is built primarily of mass timber, straw and other biogenic materials, and the landscape design aims to increase the site's biodiversity by 10%. More than 40% of the site is dedicated to landscape: an artificial wetland surrounds the building, and a cultivated meadow covers the 30,000 m² roof. The project is under construction.

The project was presented in two lectures of the studio by Henning Larsen: on its low-carbon structure and materials (Simon Ingvartsen) and on its landscape and biodiversity (Franziska Meisel). The insights from both lectures are linked below.

Sources: [[Henning Larsen, BESTSELLER Logistics Center West]], [[Big Box, Small Footprint – Lessons from Bestseller's Low-Carbon Logistics Campus]], [[Nature positive by design – Creating lasting impact by designing for and with nature]].
