## Project

Chapelle International is a logistics hotel ("hôtel logistique") in the new Chapelle International eco-district in Paris 18e, built by Sogaris and designed by the architects A26. Sogaris describes it as its first logistics hotel in Greater Paris and the first example of a new asset class. The building was inaugurated in 2018 and has 41,500 m² of floor area. Its 15,000 m² logistics ground floor keeps the capacity to host internalised rail freight operations in the future.

The other activities on the site serve the neighbourhood: sports pitches, an urban farm, a restaurant and a fitness club. A district heating plant fed by the energy of a data centre heats the logistics hotel and buildings of the neighbourhood. The project won the MIPIM Awards 2018 in the category "best industrial and logistics development".
