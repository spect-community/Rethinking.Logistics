## Project

Nexus Logistics Park is a logistics park near Dublin developed and managed by IPUT Real Estate Dublin. It is located by the M2 and M50 motorways, 10 minutes from Dublin Airport and the Dublin Port Tunnel. The developer describes 17 logistics units with 2.5 million sq ft of warehousing on 182 acres of land. Units 1–9 form the first phase, and Units 10–17 are labelled Phase 2. Unit Four was ready for occupation in April 2026.

According to the developer, every unit can be built with a glue-laminated timber (glulam) frame instead of steel, and each unit "has the potential to be delivered to a net zero carbon standard". The units target LEED Gold and BER A1 and have a 14 m clear internal height. They also have roof-mounted photovoltaic panels, NZEB-compliant fabric and MEP design, sub-metering, LED lighting with daylight harvesting, and rainwater harvesting. Smart metering monitors electricity use, warehouse temperature and indoor air quality.

The park is described as "set on a 64-acre greenway" and planted with 40,000 native trees and shrubs. It includes an indoor gym, a full-size astro pitch, two padel courts, cycling and jogging paths, and a central pavilion. The developer does not explain how the 64-acre greenway relates to the 182-acre site.

- Net zero carbon units through timber as an alternative to steel
- On-site renewable energy and energy monitoring
- Placemaking and well-being for the people who work in the park
