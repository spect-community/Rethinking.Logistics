## Project

The Ricola Kräuterzentrum (Herb Centre) in Laufen, Switzerland, is a hall where Ricola stores and processes herbs, designed by Herzog & de Meuron (project 2010–2013, realisation 2013–2014). It is 111 m long, 29 m wide and 11 m high, with three levels and 4,800 m² of gross floor area on a 15,354 m² site.

The building is built largely out of locally sourced earth. The prefabricated rammed earth elements are made in a nearby factory from loam, marl and material excavated on site, compacted in formwork and layered in blocks. A trass-lime mortar layer every eight layers of earth resists erosion by wind and rain. The self-supporting façade is linked to the concrete loadbearing structure inside. According to the architects, earth regulates humidity and so helps energy use and climate control; photovoltaic modules on the roof and waste heat from the nearby production centre also contribute. The rammed earth façade was made with the specialist Lehm Ton Erde Baukunst (Martin Rauch).
