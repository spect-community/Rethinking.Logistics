---
title: Evidence status
---

Rethinking.Logistics is published as **Edition 1.0**: the editorial work is complete. What remains open is the evidence behind individual statements — and there, the conversation is with the experts.

Every **insight** — one statement made by an expert, a student or a publication — therefore carries two labels.

## Who made the statement

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="rl-badge rl-badge--source rl-badge--source-expert">Expert</span> | Said by a practitioner or researcher in one of the studio's expert lectures. Unless marked as a verbatim quote, it is *paraphrased from the lecture*. |
| <span class="rl-badge rl-badge--source rl-badge--source-student">Student</span> | Stated by a student in a portfolio or questionnaire. Numbers are *as reported by the student*. |
| <span class="rl-badge rl-badge--source rl-badge--source-external">External</span> | Taken from a published report, standard or paper, which is linked as the source. |

## Where the evidence stands

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="rl-badge rl-badge--evidence rl-badge--evidence-open-for-review">Open for expert review</span> | Recorded faithfully from its source and open for experts to confirm, refine or challenge. This is the starting point for every statement in Edition 1.0. |
| <span class="rl-badge rl-badge--evidence rl-badge--evidence-expert-reviewed">Expert-reviewed</span> | An expert has reviewed the statement; their name is given under *Reviewed by*. |
| <span class="rl-badge rl-badge--evidence rl-badge--evidence-disputed">Disputed</span> | An expert or another source points the other way. The statement stays visible, so the discussion is visible too. |

## Editorial status is something else

The *status* shown at the top of every page (for example *published*) is about our editing — whether the page is finished. The evidence status above is about the claim itself. A page can be fully published while the statements on it remain open for expert review.

## Where to find the source

Each insight names its source (a lecture, questionnaire or publication) and, where possible, *where in the source* it was made — a timestamp, transcript line or page number.

Are you an expert on one of the topics? See [[How to contribute]] to confirm or correct a statement.
