## The conflict

Relocatable blocks, inflatable envelopes and façades open to the public adapt easily and touch the site lightly. They insulate less, are less stable and add transport when moved, so flexibility is traded against thermal and structural performance.

## In the student projects

- [[Amy Naprstek]] — Low thermal insulation of the lightweight relocatable blocks (thermal comfort, ability to store climate-sensitive goods) is traded against lower operational cost and lower embodied/operational carbon (questionnaire).
- [[Amy Naprstek]] — Relocatability and low site impact (area covered, biodiversity gain) come at the cost of truck transport emissions for moving the blocks, which the student has not yet quantified (questionnaire 4.4).
- [[Elsa Hauksdottir]] — An open façade for public permeability and daylight conflicts with the thermal performance needed in compact, closed logistics mode, so the façade switches between the two modes.
- [[Elsa Hauksdottir]] — A fully pneumatic envelope gives the most spatial flexibility, while a hybrid with only the roof inflatable trades some of that for structural stability.
