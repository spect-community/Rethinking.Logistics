## The conflict

In energy-symbiosis projects, more servers mean more waste heat to share with neighbours. They also raise energy and water demand, take the place of more labour-intensive uses, and still need full cooling capacity for the hottest day.

## In the student projects

- [[Julia Godard Lombard]] — Raising the data-centre share increases heat production, recoverable heat and heat-recovery radius, but lowers the jobs created (social impact) as sauna and dry-food production are removed by P3.
- [[Julia Godard Lombard]] — More servers raise recoverable heat but also raise energy consumption and water consumption, which grow with the data-centre share.

## What experts say

- [[Data centres are giant heaters]] — They must hold ~100% uptime through the hottest summer day, so they need chiller capacity sized for the worst case regardless of waste-heat recovery.
