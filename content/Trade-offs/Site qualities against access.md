## The conflict

Site choice weighs climate and place qualities, such as wind for passive cooling or a waterfront, against access for construction vehicles, trucks and people.

## In the student projects

- [[Robin Jesenský]] — Wind speed drives site choice, but the windiest candidate sites were protected or hard to reach for heavy construction vehicles (questionnaire, location alternatives).
- [[Sara Sulollari]] — The Borgartún location alternative gave better access but lost the waterfront connection and industrial identity; the waterfront site was chosen despite worse wind exposure (questionnaire).
