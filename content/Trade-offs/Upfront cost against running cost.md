## The conflict

Better envelopes, new infrastructure and added structure raise the initial investment. The projects accept this in exchange for lower energy use and running costs over the life of the building, sometimes with a stated payback period.

## In the student projects

- [[Adam Večeřa]] — Higher CapEx share (50% vs 40%) is accepted to reduce OpEx by 60% over 30 years, with a 12-year payback (life-cycle cost KPI).
- [[Johannes Berger]] — A drone network needs very high upfront CapEx for new infrastructure but promises lower OpEx, especially for last-mile delivery (questionnaire Economy 1).
- [[Eva Kažukolovaitė]] — Better long-term energy performance of the hub requires higher upfront costs and takes space, reducing space efficiency (operational energy vs. CapEx and space efficiency) (questionnaire).
- [[Hynek Hrabík]] — CapEx rises for extra corridor structures and conveyor technology, possibly offset by less paved area and lower embodied carbon in floors (questionnaire).
