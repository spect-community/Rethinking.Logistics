## The conflict

Cargo bikes, fewer docks and compact bays cut paved area and emissions. They also reduce capacity at peak demand or demand more precise operation, unless the layout is redesigned around them.

## In the student projects

- [[Khrystyna Verbitska]] — Prioritising cargo-bike deliveries lowers CO2 emissions and urban traffic impact but reduces throughput and scalability, especially at peak demand (emissions vs. logistics capacity).
- [[Hynek Hrabík]] — Removing docks cuts paved surface, but in a conventional layout it reduces throughput (40 classic docks bottleneck at about 245 vs about 250). Only the flow-through layout keeps throughput with fewer docks.
- [[Hynek Hrabík]] — Bays without buffer zones and visual guides demand more precise truck manoeuvring and reduce flexibility, an operational risk traded against footprint (questionnaire 4.4).
