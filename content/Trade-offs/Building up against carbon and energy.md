## The conflict

Stacking logistics in height reduces the footprint and frees land. Taller structures need more material and more energy to run, so land use is traded against carbon and cost.

- [[Johannes Berger]] — Increasing warehouse height reduces area footprint (land use) but increases embodied carbon and CapEx (questionnaire; student says both extremes are developed).
