## What it measures

How many jobs each square metre of a logistics site does at once — whether nature is blended into the programme or left as single-purpose decoration.

## Calculation

1. Split all surfaces (ground, roof, facade) into zones.
2. For each zone, count how many of these functions it serves: logistics operation, stormwater handling, habitat for target species, human amenity (breaks, walks), energy generation.
3. Index = Σ(zone area × number of functions) ÷ total surface area.

Simpler variant: % of total surface area serving two or more functions.

Can be done in Grasshopper by tagging surfaces with function attributes and summing tagged areas.

## Source

Franziska Meisel (Henning Larsen), lecture *Nature positive by design*. On Bestseller Logistics: the wetland "doesn't only serve the purpose of biodiversity… it has this multifunctional kind of purpose" (rainwater, air, user experience); the roof meadow gives "space for lunch break… always kind of blending nature with the function"; "every surface, both roof and facade, is also used to really gain more biodiversity" — while the logistics programme still has to work.
