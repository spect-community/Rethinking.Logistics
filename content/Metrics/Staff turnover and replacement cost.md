## What it measures

How often warehouse staff leave and what it costs to replace them.

## Calculation

- Turnover rate = separations per year ÷ average headcount × 100
- Annual replacement cost = separations per year × cost per replacement (recruitment, onboarding, lost productivity)

Illustrative example: 200 staff × 40 % turnover × €3,000 per replacement = €240,000 per year.

## Reported values

- Direct replacement cost of about $3,000–5,000 per warehouse worker (US industry sources; no European figure found).
- Annual average monthly separations rate in US transportation, warehousing and utilities of about 4 % (US Bureau of Labor Statistics).

## Evidence

The link between workplace design and lower turnover is supported by surveys ([[Savills – What Warehouse Workers Want (2020)]]) but not by controlled studies. Treat it as a hypothesis.
