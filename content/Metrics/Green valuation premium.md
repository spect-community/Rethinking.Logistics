## What it measures

How much more investors value certified assets than comparable non-certified ones.

## Calculation

Cap rate of comparable non-certified assets − cap rate of certified assets.

## Reference values

BREEAM-certified logistics assets in the UK and Europe valued 25 bps higher than non-certified ones (CBRE Investment Management, cited by ULI).
