## What it measures

The usable height for racking and automation, and one of the first things a tenant asks for.

## Calculation

Distance from finished floor to the lowest overhead obstruction (structure, sprinklers, lighting).

## Reference values

Modern bulk distribution needs about 11.0–12.2 m (36–40 ft) for racking and automation. Older stock has about 7.3–9.8 m (24–32 ft). An 11 m building stores about 50 % more pallets than a 7.3 m building of the same footprint.
