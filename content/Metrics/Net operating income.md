## What it measures

The base of value. Sustainability reaches it mainly through rent, occupancy and new income (PV, charging), not through owner-side savings under triple-net leases.

## Calculation

Gross revenue (rent + recoveries + other income) − operating expenses. Excludes financing, depreciation and capital expenditure.
