## What it measures

Electricity the roofs could produce. Large flat warehouse roofs make this one of the strongest levers of logistics buildings.

## Calculation

Usable roof area × annual solar irradiation (kWh/m²) × module efficiency × performance ratio. Irradiation from solar radiation analysis (Ladybug, Cyclops) or PVGIS.
