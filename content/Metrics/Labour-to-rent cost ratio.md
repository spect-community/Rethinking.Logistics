## What it measures

How much a logistics operator spends on staff compared with rent for the building.

## Calculation

Annual labour cost on site ÷ annual rent

## Reported values

- Users of logistics real estate spend about $5–7 on labour and $10 on transport for every $1 of rent; rent is about 5 % of supply chain costs ([[Prologis – Unlocking Supply Chain Value (2018)]], industry research, mainly US data).

## Why it matters

If labour costs several times more than rent, a building that helps attract and keep staff can pay back its extra cost through the operator, even when the developer does not see the benefit. This is a hypothesis to test, see [[Staff turnover and replacement cost]].

None. Requires operator data.
