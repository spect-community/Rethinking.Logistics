## What it measures

Greenhouse-gas emissions caused by producing and building the structure, per square metre.

## Calculation

Material quantities × emission factors from EPDs/databases, summed over life-cycle modules (A1–A3 or A1–C4) ÷ gross floor area. Calculated with LCA tools such as One Click LCA or Bombyx.
