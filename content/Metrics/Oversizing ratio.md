## What it measures

How much capacity the building provides beyond what its user actually needs.

## Calculation

(Provided capacity − required capacity) ÷ required capacity × 100

Calculated separately for each system:

- floor area and clear height
- floor load capacity
- number of docks and yard area
- car and truck parking
- heating, cooling and ventilation capacity

## Data needed

The required capacity comes from a tenant brief, operational data or post-occupancy metering. In speculative development the tenant is unknown at design stage, so the ratio can only be calculated after occupation or against a reference operation.
