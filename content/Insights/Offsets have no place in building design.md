## Context

Pavel Fojtík, referring to frameworks such as the UK's net-zero carbon building standard, asked whether carbon credits and offsetting will become an integral part of the business. Paul Astle (Ramboll) rejects offsetting at building level, while allowing that trading may be needed at material level (he mentions the existing ETS).

## From the transcript

> I don't think it should be or can be a part of building design because I don't think it's necessary either. I think it would be very confusing and the danger is that suddenly it becomes much cheaper to buy credit than it does to design a building well and if I go back to that building from [Eladio Dieste] that was designed because of a constraint. If instead at that time [you'd] been able to buy your way out of it through other ways, then you would never have got that building and you'd never [have] got that solution. […] I think we can solve this problem through design, clever design, good use of materials.
