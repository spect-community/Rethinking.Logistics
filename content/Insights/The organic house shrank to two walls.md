## Context

Vele (CTU Prague / Scoolpt) tells how the design of Prvok changed. The founder of the company had drawn it as a living organism resembling a protozoan (hence the Czech name Prvok), but test prints of its sections kept collapsing where the curve was too steep or the wall too flat.

## From the transcript

> He drew this beautiful kind of like a living organism, which looked like a protozoa. […] So in the end when we did these several testing grounds, we ended up with much simplified design. And you can see that in the final section only these two like the walls were 3D printed and the top room was made of wood. […] Please do notice this part. It's an elliptic window which at the top where you have like these layers that are stacking on top of each other and slightly like overlapping and then creating an overhang. We had to support it somehow because when you are printing it's liquid so it would like fall down directly. So we put a [slew] of foam layers and put them on top of each other to avoid the layers to collapse. This was done manually and it was kind of annoying, but in the end we succeeded.
