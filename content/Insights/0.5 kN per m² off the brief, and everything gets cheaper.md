## Context

A student asked Paul Astle (Ramboll) how to prioritise decarbonisation when the budget is tight. Astle says early interventions at the brief stage can save cost and carbon at once. His example is a very tall flagship building under construction in London, where the team persuaded the client to accept a lower design floor load.

## From the transcript

> […] in terms of prioritisation, it's always got to be finding a good solution that doesn't cost more and we really should be pushing for that. So for example, in a really tall building in London, the flagship building, it's been built right now. Normally the design load would have been 3 kilonewtons per square meter for the [floors] and we managed to convince the client that they could go down to 2.5 and it might not sound like a lot, but straight away you've reduced the amount of load in that building, so everything is going to be cheaper immediately because we've saved some load.
