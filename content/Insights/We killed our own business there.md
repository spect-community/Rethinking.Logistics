## Context

Lukáš Janáč and Gregory M. Speck (YUAR Architects, Prague) on the end result of seven years of work for the Penny supermarket chain. YUAR wrote a design manual that lets Penny build its stores across Czechia without them. Only the permit documentation, which must be authorised for each site, still needs an architect.
