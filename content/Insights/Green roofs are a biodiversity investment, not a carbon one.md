## Context

A student researching green roofs asked Paul Astle (Ramboll) how worthwhile they are for absorbing carbon on a logistics centre, together with planting trees around it. Astle calls it one of the difficult trade-offs of integrating biodiversity into buildings.

## From the transcript

> […] inevitably to do that you actually need to support quite a lot of mass to make it work […] So if you had a logistics centre that has a single Storey and it has a large span roof, it's probably not going to make sense overall in my opinion to use a green roof because you would massively increase the mass of that roof and at that kind of span that would really increase the structure that would be necessary […] But if you had a building where the column centres were quite narrow and you already had a multi-storey situation, then it might make more sense to have localised green roof areas […] But a green roof doesn't really help with carbon.
