## Context

Signe Kongebro (Ramboll) continues with 'Making a Pact with Nature', an 18-hectare new district in Copenhagen, split into three small neighbourhoods so nature can run through the site. The ground is a former dump site.

## From the transcript

> And because it was, or is, an old dump site, we cannot pile in the ground, we cannot dig, we cannot have any parking basements. We said, OK, we need to actually make these islands into real islands. So there's a 2 meters difference between each of the neighborhood[s] and the green area in the middle. And that was part of also the water management strategy. So every time you have that sort of rain coming, that would actually be just part of the more wetland in the middle and then you would keep your feet dry on the islands, and then finally creating one ring [road] where you could lead the traffic around, but not having any parking, very limited parking in the neighborhoods, and having parking houses close to the main road outside of the area.
