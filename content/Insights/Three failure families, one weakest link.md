## Context

Vele (CTU Prague / Scoolpt) wraps up the first part of the lecture, on building Prvok, by naming the three groups of problems the construction revealed; they became the starting point of his PhD research.

## From the transcript

> So summing up, wrapping up this part of the [Prvok], we during the construction and printing realized there are like 3 big groups of issues. The first one is that when you are slicing something which has like more curved shapes, you are getting the containment problem where the actual printed object isn't the same as the digital twin because the layers kind of create these stair stepping effects […] The other one, as I already mentioned, is the early age deformation, that when you do print, you can very easily come to the place where it collapses […] And then in the final stage the issue is that the material is very anisotropic. So it's strong inside the layers, but the bond between the layers is weak. And when it should crack, it always cracks or most often cracks inside this layer connection. So these layer connections are the weakest spots of the whole printing.
