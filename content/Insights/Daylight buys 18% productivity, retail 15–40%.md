## What needs checking

A verification pass against public sources (claim bank, fact-check item 12) found:

**Misattributed — the report does not make these claims.** It deliberately avoids single headline productivity percentages. What it *does* give: view quality + daylight explain 6.5% of variation in sick leave; 4% performance drop at cooler and 6% at warmer temperatures; 8–11% productivity gains linked to air quality; "single digit improvements" from personal temperature control. The **40%** retail figure is real but belongs to Heschong Mahone (1999) — skylit stores likely to have 40% higher sales (range 31–49%). The 18% and 23% figures do not appear.
