## Context

Jakub Veselka (EY), an architect by training, explains the physical-risk side of a climate risk assessment: which risks a building's location is exposed to, what financial loss or extra investment they may cause, and finally an adaptation plan. He points out that the plan applies to existing buildings, and to new ones already at the design stage.

## From the transcript

> On top of that, once we know which climate risks are relevant for our building, we can evaluate probability of financial losses or of additional financial investments related to this specific building. […] And in the end, if we put the whole climate physical risk assessment together, we can create something called adaptation plan, which means a plan how to adapt those buildings to climate change. And this is something which you can do with the current buildings. Or if you design your own building, you can count or take into account your adaptation measures within your design phase.
