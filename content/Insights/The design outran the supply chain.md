## Context

Signe Kongebro (Ramboll) presents World of Volvo in Gothenburg, Volvo's global brand centre within its heritage area. It was meant to be the most sustainable building in the city and is a round building whose roof is carried by three 'mushroom' cores.

## From the transcript

> So the idea here was to say how can we create one, you can say, brand cathedral for the brand Volvo. […] we wanted to create a project for the future talking about how can you create the most sustainable building in Gothenburg and make a beacon that talks about them transforming into a mobility provider for this future, and how we did that was coming up with a concept of a biogenic building.
