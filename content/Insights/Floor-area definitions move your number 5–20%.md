Discussing how carbon results are normalised, Paul Astle (Ramboll) notes that they are most often given per square metre of floor area, and warns about how that area is defined.

## From the transcript

> So I'm sure you've come across kilograms of CO2 per square meter, which is fine, but it's super important that we understand what the floor area definition is. We have, believe it or not, across the world, dozens of different ways of defining floor area. In some countries it's up to the face of the external wall.
