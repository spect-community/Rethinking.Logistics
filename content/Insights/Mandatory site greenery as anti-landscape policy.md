## Context

A student asked who pays for the infrastructure a brownfield needs. Pavel Sovička (Panattoni CZ & SK) adds his criticism of the Czech rule that industrial sites keep 20–30% of their area green, compared with Germany and the US, and of a municipality's demand for greenery on a former steel mill.

## From the transcript

> In Czech, most of the industrial sites are having a regulation that you have to keep 20 to 30% green, which is not helping anyone, but we have to consume more space to accommodate the same building. [Than] in Germany or in US, we need 30% more space in [Czech] of land to accommodate it. So in a lot of cases when we utilize, for example, old steel mill which was all concrete, we are asked by the municipalities: here, but create 30% of the green area. Which is like complete nonsense, because you create less industrial or more space. You want to just get it as dense as possible so you don't consume more free space outside the city, right?
