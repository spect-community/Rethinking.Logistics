## Context

Pavel Sovička (Panattoni CZ & SK) says Panattoni was one of the first Czech industrial developers to focus on brownfields, many of which had significant environmental issues. He compares the Czech rules for contaminated sites with the German and English approach his company reviewed.

## From the transcript

> We were one of the first industrial developers in Czech which focused on brownfield utilisation. […] A lot of the brownfield sites in the Czech Republic had significant environmental issues. The code is really strict. I would say it's really much stricter than in the Western countries; [we were] reviewing the German approach, English approach to utilisation of the brownfields, and we were shocked how strict and difficult it is. I think the only more difficult location in Europe is Poland […] So it's very difficult to recycle the material on site, despite the fact it's pretty safe and it doesn't really bring any harm to the future in terms of contamination. A lot of that you have to take away. It's very costly [to] store it somewhere else, and it's very difficult discussion with the authorities.
