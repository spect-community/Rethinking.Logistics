## Context

Opening his lecture, Paul Astle, decarbonisation lead at Ramboll Buildings, places carbon within the wider sustainability agenda and responds to the 'carbon tunnel vision' critique.

## From the transcript

> So some of you may have come across this before or heard of this idea of having carbon tunnel vision. That's this idea that if we only focus on carbon, we will stop looking at all these other issues that we need to consider in the wider sustainability space. And that might be to do with water, health, education, inequality. […] However, I see that as a different perspective because actually what I find is that when we look at carbon of materials or indeed of buildings, it actually can raise attention to those things because they're so interlinked in terms of the fact that you need to understand the source of materials, you need to understand the source of energy, that asks bigger questions and you can reach out to address those issues. […] I see it as a gateway for conversations [on] wider sustainability issues […]
