## Context

Advice from Lukáš Janáč and Gregory M. Speck (YUAR Architects, Prague) to the students: the decision-makers behind logistics and corporate projects do not think about architecture but about decisions and problems. An architect who does not speak that language joins the process too late.

> So what's important for you later in your job to understand as an [architect] is that these people don't solve architecture. They solve decisions and problem solving.
