## Context

Parker (Oxford Economics) links rising AI use to the rapid growth of data centres in Europe, which has so far lagged behind the US and China, and describes where they have been built. ('Flat cities' in the transcript is FLAP.) He adds that the Nordic cities are supported by renewable energy, a cooler climate and strong infrastructure.
