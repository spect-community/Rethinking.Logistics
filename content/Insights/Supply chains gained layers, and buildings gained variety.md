## Context

Cartlich (CBRE) explains how supply chains have become more complicated, partly but not only because of e-commerce, comparing the old linear chain from manufacturer to store with today's parallel models.

## From the transcript

> So in the old days you had manufacturers who would ship their products to a national distribution centre which would then be shipped to or driven generally to a regional distribution centre and then that would be distributed to […] stores and then consumers would come along and buy them. Nowadays that is much more complicated. […] there's a cross [dock] which is a […] sort of intermediary distribution centre where trucks come in and vans go out. And […] that fleet of vans delivers to consumers, you might miss out one level of that supply chain, the regional distribution centre and go straight to vans. Or you might even go straight from a distribution centre […] and this would be the case for the likes of Amazon or [Zalando] or ASOS and you might go direct to consumer […]. So there are a number of different models now for the supply chains and that means you have more demand for space, you have more variety of buildings.
