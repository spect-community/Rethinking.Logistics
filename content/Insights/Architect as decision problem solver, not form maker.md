## Context

Lukáš Janáč and Gregory M. Speck (YUAR Architects, Prague) contrast the popular image of the architect as a form maker in a black T-shirt with their own work on logistics and manufacturing halls and large offices. They describe that work as solving decision problems with calculations and studies.
