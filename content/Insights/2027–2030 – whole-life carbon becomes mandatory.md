**One correction.** Art. 7 EPBD (EU) 2024/1275: roadmap for GWP limit values by 2027; from **1 Jan 2028 life-cycle GWP must be calculated and disclosed for all new buildings over 1,000 m²** — the 2028 public-buildings date is the *zero-emission* obligation, a different thing; from 1 Jan 2030 for all new buildings.
