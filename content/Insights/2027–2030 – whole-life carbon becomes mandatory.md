In the life-cycle assessment (LCA) part of the lecture, the UCEEB researchers (CTU Prague) outline the EU legislative timeline for building carbon under the recast Energy Performance of Buildings Directive (EPBD).

## What needs checking

A verification pass against public sources (claim bank, fact-check item 6) found:

**One correction.** Art. 7 EPBD (EU) 2024/1275: roadmap for GWP limit values by 2027; from **1 Jan 2028 life-cycle GWP must be calculated and disclosed for all new buildings over 1,000 m²** — the 2028 public-buildings date is the *zero-emission* obligation, a different thing; from 1 Jan 2030 for all new buildings. Netherlands confirmed: MPG limit of 1.0 binding from 1 Jan 2018, tightened to 0.8 for dwellings in July 2021.
