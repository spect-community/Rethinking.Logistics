## Context

František Novotný (CREA-TURA) presents the multi-storey food (cake, biscuit and snack-bar) factory in the centre of Frýdek-Místek, built on a former textile-factory brownfield next to housing, a school and the river. Because the site is small and in the city centre, production has to be stacked on several floors, which he contrasts with the usual Czech factory.

## From the transcript

> You are not on the green field somewhere behind the city, you are in the city center. You have to build layers. You have to build layers for the manufacturing, which is not very common because once you are manufacturing goods, whatever goods those are, you need machines, which are heavy, very heavy, and making those buildings with stories is very new in Czech Republic because every time something like this is happening, it should be vast on one floor and it should be all covered with no gaps, with no incisions, with nothing. So the manufacturer is unbothered by the environment outside. We didn't want to do that. The client didn't want to do that. […] The client was from the beginning set [on] it in this way. He wants to do it newly, not as it is done every time.
