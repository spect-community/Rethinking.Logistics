## Context

A student asked how strong the incentive is for developers to add trees, green space and biodiversity to Czech logistics parks for the workers. Lukáš Janáč and Gregory M. Speck (YUAR Architects, Prague) answer that in logistics there is none. In offices it is growing, because employers need to bring people back to the office after COVID.
