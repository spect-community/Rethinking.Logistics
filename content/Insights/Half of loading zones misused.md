## Context

Wiebke Müller (EIT Urban Mobility) presents the Smart Zone Enforcement Module, an EIT Urban Mobility co-funded project, as an example of curbside management. She adds that curb rules and enforcement differ strongly from country to country, so the solution has to be tested in different cities to scale.
