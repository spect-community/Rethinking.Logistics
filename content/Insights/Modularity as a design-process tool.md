## Context

František Novotný (CREA-TURA), presenting the multi-storey food (cake, biscuit and snack-bar) factory in the centre of Frýdek-Místek, built on a former textile-factory brownfield next to housing, a school and the river, turns to the questions asked in the design process — whether the building is modular, reusable, low in CO2 — and explains what modular prefabricated construction offers during the years-long design of a factory.

## From the transcript

> And right now we know that we can do modular buildings. We can do them very easy. We can order everything. It can be prefab.
