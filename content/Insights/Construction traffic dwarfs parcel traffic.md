## Quote

> "last mile delivery actually only accounts for about 3% of the vehicle kilometers driven. But construction side logistics accounts for 37%."

## What needs checking

A verification pass against public sources (claim bank, fact-check item 7) found:

**Both need trimming.** Best-sourced last-mile figure is **41%** of overall supply chain cost (Capgemini, 20,000-consumer study); 60% has no primary source. Construction logistics: the standard peer-reviewed claim is **"up to 30% of freight movements in cities"** — 37% is unsupported, and it is *movements*, not vehicle-kilometres. [Capgemini](https://www.capgemini.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Report-Digital-%E2%80%93-Last-Mile-Delivery-Challenge1.pdf) · [Guerlain et al.](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/11/21/6118)
