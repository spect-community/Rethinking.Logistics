## Context

In the Q&A a student asked about electric trucks. James Fitzgerald (IO Partners) says he is never asked about electric or hydrogen trucks, only about EV charging for workers' cars, and describes how developers handle it. Asked why he does not promote them, he says demand has to come from tenants and, in the end, consumers.
