## What needs checking

A verification pass against public sources (claim bank, fact-check item 7) found:

**Both need trimming.** Best-sourced last-mile figure is **41%** of overall supply chain cost (Capgemini, 20,000-consumer study); 60% has no primary source. Construction logistics: the standard peer-reviewed claim is **"up to 30% of freight movements in cities"** — 37% is unsupported, and it is *movements*, not vehicle-kilometres.
