## Externally checked

A verification pass against public sources (claim bank, fact-check item 13) found:

**Confirmed.** CEE/European standard has moved from 10 m to 12 m clear height. 30 m is comfortably permitted in Poland — Żabka's automated centre at Radzymin near Warsaw has a **40 m** high-bay warehouse. Polish regulations class 25–55 m as "wysokie" with no prohibition at 30 m. [Property Forum](https://www.property-forum.eu/news/new-warehouses-to-be-more-economical-and-user-friendly/9010)
