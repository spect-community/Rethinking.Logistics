## Context

František Novotný (CREA-TURA) presents the multi-storey food (cake, biscuit and snack-bar) factory in the centre of Frýdek-Místek, built on a former textile-factory brownfield next to housing, a school and the river, which replaces an operating plant step by step. He explains why the phases had to be fixed at the start of the design.

## From the transcript

> Once you are building, for example, this part, this has to work, cannot be interrupted. So the phasing is very, very tricky, not just the design itself. And this phase one is for 10 years, 15 years. Then […] you will do the phase two, then you do the phase three.
