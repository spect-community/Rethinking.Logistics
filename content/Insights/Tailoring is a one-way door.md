## Context

A student asked whether one typology (build-to-suit, build-to-own, build-to-lease, data centres) is more versatile than the others. Pavel Sovička (Panattoni CZ & SK) opens his answer with buildings tailor-made for heavily automated tenants such as Amazon or H&M; his first sentence reads in the transcript as if such tailoring were the aim, probably with a 'not' missing, since he goes on to say customers should 'not go crazy'.

## From the transcript

> You know, what we are trying to achieve is that the buildings which we deliver and the space we deliver to our customers is tailor-made to the point where it's not usable for the future. […] So what we are trying to convince our customers is that to have certain modification of the buildings, but not go crazy. Of course you can't tell it to Amazon, which is investing into the technology, or H&M, which is extremely expensive. It's multiples of the building's value, right? And we have to follow them and we build it around it. The chance that the building in 20 years, because most of those [leases] are 20 years, that it will be utilized in the same way by different tenant, it's very limited. So we always have to take care that in the future it will be possible to recycle it very easily, on site ideally.
