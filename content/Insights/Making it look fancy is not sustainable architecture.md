## Context

Looking back on their office-interior work, Lukáš Janáč and Gregory M. Speck (YUAR Architects, Prague) say they used to just make offices look fancy. Their current view of sustainable design is based on how people use space and on the real life of an office fit-out, about a 10-year lease.

> Because what we did, especially in office design, we would come in and we could just make it look fancy. And nowadays we understand that this is not the way sustainable architecture is done.
