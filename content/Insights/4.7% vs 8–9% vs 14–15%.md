## What needs checking

A verification pass against public sources (claim bank, fact-check item 10) found:

**Do not publish as a clean comparison** — the three numbers come from three different definitions. Eurostat LUCAS (2022) gives EU artificial land 4.4%, Netherlands 13.0%, Belgium 12.0%; no Czechia or Germany figure is published in those tables. Germany's often-quoted 14.5% is Destatis "settlement and transport area", a wider definition; the Czech land register's "built-up area and courtyards" is only ~1.7%.
