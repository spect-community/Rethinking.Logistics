## Context

Continuing on the fit-out of the printed house Prvok, Vele (CTU Prague / Scoolpt) explains why parts designed from the digital model did not fit the built walls.

## From the transcript

> Even though we had the whole model in Rhino, we kind of expected that we can export the curvatures for shelves and other parts from Rhino and they will fit. But unfortunately it wasn't the true because when you are printing, the material is, as I said, still kind of liquid and it deforms. It doesn't collapse completely, but it slightly deforms. And these deformations, there were like 2 to 5 centimeters of deformations which are not that much visible by eye. But when you put like the exact curve that was exported from Rhino, it doesn't fit. So we had to like remake the whole desk of the kitchen according to actual curvature, not to the one that was in Rhino, which was kind of a setback.
