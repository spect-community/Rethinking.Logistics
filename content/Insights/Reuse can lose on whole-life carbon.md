## Context

A student whose project converts abandoned buildings into data centres asked Paul Astle (Ramboll) why new build could be better than renovation for carbon. Astle explains the trade-off between upfront and operational carbon when reusing a building.

## From the transcript

> So using existing buildings is really good for embodied carbon because we don't have to spend as much embodied carbon because we've already got the structure and all the walls and we can reuse those directly, which is great.
