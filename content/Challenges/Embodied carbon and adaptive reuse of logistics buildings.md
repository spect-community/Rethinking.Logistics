## Challenge

Building new warehouses produces large embodied emissions; demolishing old ones wastes the carbon already spent. Material choice, lighter structures and adaptive reuse are the main levers.

## Design questions

- What are the embodied emissions per m² of each design option?
- How much carbon does reusing the existing building save?
- Which elements and life-cycle stages dominate the footprint?
