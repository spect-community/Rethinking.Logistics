## Challenge

The last step of delivery is the most expensive and polluting part of the supply chain. Micro-hubs, cargo bikes, consolidation and new modes like drones can cut trips, emissions and traffic.

## Design questions

- How many vehicle-km and emissions does the delivery model cause?
- How large is the catchment of one hub, and how many hubs does a city need?
- How fast can parcels reach customers?
