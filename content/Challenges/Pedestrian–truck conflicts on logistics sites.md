## Challenge

Where trucks, vans, workers and the public meet, collisions are the main risk — especially when logistics opens up to the city.

## Design questions

- Where do pedestrian and vehicle routes cross?
- Are hazardous processes separated from people?
- How safe are workers in operational areas?
