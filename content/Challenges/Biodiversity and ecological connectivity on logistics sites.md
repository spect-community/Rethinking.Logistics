## Challenge

Logistics sites often cut ecological corridors and replace habitats with asphalt. Green roofs, planting and connected landscapes can turn them into contributors to biodiversity.

## Design questions

- Does the project increase biodiversity compared with the baseline?
- Does site greenery connect to corridors and water bodies?
- Which native species does the design support?
