## Challenge

Logistics sites take large urban areas but give little back. Roofs, edges and leftover spaces can become parks, plazas or community rooms — if access, safety and operations are designed together.

## Design questions

- How much of the site can be opened to the public?
- Will the public spaces actually be used?
- What do local communities gain?
