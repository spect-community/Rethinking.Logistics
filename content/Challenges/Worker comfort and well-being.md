## Challenge

Logistics workers often spend shifts in deep, dark, noisy halls. Daylight, air, acoustics, safety and rest spaces shape their health and the attractiveness of the jobs.

## Design questions

- How much daylight reaches the workplaces?
- Are noise and air quality acceptable?
- What facilities do workers have?
