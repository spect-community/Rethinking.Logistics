## Challenge

Warehouses are among the most land-hungry building types: one storey, huge footprints, large paved yards and parking. Reducing land take means stacking, compacting or sharing.

## Design questions

- How much land does the logistics function consume per unit of output?
- Can the footprint be reduced by stacking or better yard design?
- What can the freed land be used for?
