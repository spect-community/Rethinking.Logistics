## Challenge

Logistics changes faster than buildings: tenants, automation and delivery models change within years. Adaptable, modular and relocatable structures avoid premature demolition.

## Design questions

- How quickly can the building be reconfigured or extended?
- Can it be taken apart and rebuilt elsewhere?
- What does flexibility cost?
