## About this source

Industry-funded study (Tata Steel and BCSA) modelling a 35,400 m² distribution warehouse in Stoke-on-Trent. Dated (2009 prices).

Capital cost uplift for higher BREEAM ratings: +0.04 % for Very Good, +0.43 % for Excellent, +4.82 % for Outstanding (2009 prices).
