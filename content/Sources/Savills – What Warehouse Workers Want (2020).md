## About this source

Savills Netherlands survey of nearly 300 employees in the Dutch logistics sector (October 2020, published December 2020). Industry survey of stated preferences, not measured turnover.

- Investment in the workplace can help attract and retain employees.
- The largest differences are made by a healthy workplace, sports facilities and catering.
- Involving staff in the design raises satisfaction.
