## About this source

Architectural quality at scale now comes from workflow, not vision — a standardised computational toolchain, embedded PhD researchers, and an openly-governed use of AI that shifts effort from producing drawings to harvesting and interpreting data. Around that sits a materials and landscape argument: mass timber above ground, nature that residents access but don't own, and an honest account of what these choices break (basements, pavements, thermal mass, acoustics).
