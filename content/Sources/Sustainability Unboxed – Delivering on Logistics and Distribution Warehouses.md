## About this source

Industry report by the Urban Land Institute (Washington DC, 2025), written by August Williams-Eynon at the ULI Randall Lewis Center for Sustainability in Real Estate with input from the ULI Europe Logistics Council and the ULI Greenprint Industrial Subcommittee.

Industry overview of sustainability in logistics real estate, based on secondary market research and interviews with ~18 practitioners. It frames sustainability as a business case built on four drivers: asset value, tenant and investor demand, risk reduction (compliance, physical and transition risk) and community support. It covers four impact areas: decarbonisation, climate resilience, health and social equity, and nature and ecosystems. It names barriers (split incentive under triple-net leases, data collection, retrofit logistics, grid capacity, fragmented frameworks) and includes six project profiles. The report itself names the sector's information gap: implementation, measurement and benchmarking.
