## About this source

Vendor glossary of the KPIs investors and asset managers use for industrial real estate (US/REIT perspective, imperial units), accessed 2026-11-19. It lists NOI, cap rate, DSCR, operating expense ratio, square-foot yield, clear height, dock door ratio and PUE, with triple-net leasing as the standard. It gives definitions, not benchmarks. It is used here as evidence of *what investors track*, not as a data source for values.
