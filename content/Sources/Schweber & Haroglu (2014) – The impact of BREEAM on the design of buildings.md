## About this source

Peer-reviewed article by Libby Schweber and Hasan Haroglu in *Building Research & Information* (qualitative case studies, UK).

- Design teams focus on achieving BREEAM credits rather than on the building as a whole.
- The scheme tends to dictate design solutions instead of focusing on the intention behind them.

Related: Schweber, L. (2013), *The effect of BREEAM on clients and construction professionals*, *Building Research & Information*. It finds that few credits are intuitive and some requirements contradict common sense.
