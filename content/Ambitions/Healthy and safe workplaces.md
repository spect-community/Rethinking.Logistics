## Ambition

Logistics workers often spend shifts in deep, dark, noisy halls, and where trucks, vans, workers and the public meet, collisions are the main risk. Daylight, air, acoustics, rest spaces and separated routes shape their health, their safety and the attractiveness of the jobs.

## Design questions

- How much daylight reaches the workplaces?
- Where do pedestrian and vehicle routes cross?
- What facilities do workers have?
