## Ambition

Building new warehouses produces large embodied emissions, and large volumes, cooled storage and automation make them energy-intensive. Yet huge roofs can become power plants, existing buildings can be reused, and the waste heat of data centres can warm their neighbours.

## Design questions

- What are the embodied emissions per m² of each design option?
- How much of the demand can rooftop PV cover?
- How much heat does the facility produce, and who could use it?
