## Ambition

Logistics sites often cut ecological corridors, replace habitats with asphalt and seal almost all of their surface, so rainwater runs off and roofs and yards overheat. Green roofs, planting, connected landscapes and retained rainwater can turn them into contributors to biodiversity, water and outdoor comfort.

## Design questions

- Does the project increase biodiversity compared with the baseline?
- How much rainwater can be retained and reused on site?
- Do outdoor spaces get sun in winter and shade in summer?
