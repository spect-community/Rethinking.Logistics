## Ambition

Warehouses are among the most land-hungry building types, truck yards take a large share of their sites, and the last step of delivery is the most expensive and polluting part of the supply chain. Stacking, better yards, automation, micro-hubs and clean vehicles can cut land take, trips and emissions.

## Design questions

- How much land does the logistics function consume per unit of output?
- How many docks are really needed?
- How many vehicle-km and emissions does the delivery model cause?
